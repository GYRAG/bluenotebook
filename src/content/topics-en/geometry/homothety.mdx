<Definition>A **homothety** with centre $O$ and ratio $k \ne 0$ takes a point $A$ to the point $A'$ for which $\overrightarrow{OA'} = k\,\overrightarrow{OA}$. For $k > 0$, $A'$ is on the ray $OA$; for $k < 0$, on its extension on the other side of $O$.</Definition>

<Formulas>
<Formula id="homothety-formula" name="Definition" tex="\overrightarrow{OA'} = k\,\overrightarrow{OA}" live="k" proof="homothety-segment" />
<Formula id="segment-formula" name="The image of a segment" tex="A'B' = |k|\,AB, \qquad A'B' \parallel AB" live="A'B' : AB" proof="homothety-segment" />
<Formula id="area-formula" name="Area" tex="S' = k^2 S" live="S' : S" proof="homothety-area" />
</Formulas>

<Properties>

<Property id="homothety-segment" title="A homothety multiplies a segment by $|k|$ and takes it to a parallel segment" why="The triangles formed by $O$ and the ends of the segment are similar.">
<Proof given="$\overrightarrow{OA'} = k\,\overrightarrow{OA}$, $\overrightarrow{OB'} = k\,\overrightarrow{OB}$" prove="$A'B' = |k|\,AB$, $A'B' \parallel AB$">
<Step show="line:OA line:OB" hl="OA OA' OB OB'">$OA' = |k|\,OA$, $OB' = |k|\,OB$; $A'$ and $B'$ lie on the rays $OA$, $OB$ ($k > 0$) or on their extensions ($k < 0$).</Step>
<Step hl="OAB OA'B'" tex="\triangle OA'B' \sim \triangle OAB">$\tfrac{OA'}{OA} = \tfrac{OB'}{OB} = |k|$, and the angle at $O$ is common (or vertical): similar by two sides and the angle between them.</Step>
<Step hl="AB||A'B'" tex="A'B' = |k|\,AB, \qquad A'B' \parallel AB \quad \blacksquare">The similarity ratio is $|k|$; $\angle OA'B' = \angle OAB$: corresponding ($k > 0$) or alternate ($k < 0$) angles.</Step>
</Proof>
</Property>

<Property id="homothety-angles" title="A homothety takes a triangle to a similar triangle; the angles do not change" why="All three sides are multiplied by the same $|k|$.">
<Proof given="$\triangle ABC \to \triangle A'B'C'$ under a homothety" prove="$\triangle A'B'C' \sim \triangle ABC$">
<Step hl="ABC A'B'C'" tex="\frac{A'B'}{AB} = \frac{B'C'}{BC} = \frac{C'A'}{CA} = |k|">The previous property for each side.</Step>
<Step tex="\triangle A'B'C' \sim \triangle ABC \quad \blacksquare">Similar by three sides, so the corresponding angles are equal as well.</Step>
</Proof>
</Property>

<Property id="homothety-area" title="A homothety multiplies the area by $k^2$" why="The ratio of the areas of similar figures is the square of the similarity ratio.">
<Proof given="A homothety with ratio $k$" prove="$S' = k^2 S$">
<Step hl="ABC A'B'C'" tex="S' = |k|^2 S = k^2 S \quad \blacksquare">The triangles are similar with ratio $|k|$ (previous property), and the ratio of the areas is its square.</Step>
</Proof>
</Property>

</Properties>

<Remark kind="special">$k = -1$ is a point reflection; with $k = 1$ every point stays in place. A homothety followed by an isometry is called a similarity transformation: two figures are similar if one goes to the other under a similarity transformation.</Remark>

<Remark kind="mistake">With $k = -2$ the figure does not shrink: lengths grow $|k| = 2$ times, the area $k^2 = 4$ times; the sign only decides the side.</Remark>
